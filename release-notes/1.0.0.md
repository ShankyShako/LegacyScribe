## Why this update

The first release of LegacyScribe: dictation for macOS that runs on your Mac. Hold Right ⌥, talk, let go, and the text is pasted where you're typing.

## What's in it

- On-device speech-to-text: Parakeet v3 (default), Cohere Transcribe (the most accurate), Apple Speech or Whisper.
- Raw, Clean and Prompt modes, switched with ⌃⌥. Clean tidies filler words and false starts. Prompt turns a ramble into a numbered request for a coding assistant.
- Rewrites by a model downloaded to your Mac (no account needed), or a cloud AI with your own key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter), or Ollama. If the AI fails, your words are pasted as heard.
- Live caption: see your words while you speak.
- Say "Legacy," or "Lucy," first to give an instruction instead of dictating, such as rewriting the selected text.
- Notes mode: up to 2 hours hands-free, with speaker labels, editing and trimming, written up on your Mac.
- Speech Coach (optional): filler words, pace and pauses in your own recordings.
- Private mode: nothing leaves the Mac.
- Esc has Undo, and a failed transcription can be retried from History.

## Before you start

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26. Allow Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring when asked. Then, in AI Rewrite, download the recommended model or paste an API key.
